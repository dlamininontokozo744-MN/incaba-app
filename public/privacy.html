Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

Incaba is a digital tourism guide for the Kingdom of Eswatini. It helps visitors and locals discover attractions, hotels, restaurants and culture, save favourites, make bookings and orders, and ask an AI guide questions. This policy explains what information Incaba collects and how it is used. You can contact us at dlamininontokozo744@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account or sign in with email, we collect your name, email address and password (passwords are handled securely by our authentication provider). When you choose Continue with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Content you create. Favourites, bookings, orders, reviews, and business listings you add.
  • Location. If you allow it in your browser or device, your location is used to show local weather and help with navigation. You can refuse or turn this off at any time in your browser settings.
  • AI Guide messages. Questions you type to the AI Guide are sent to our AI provider so it can write a reply.
  • Device identifier. A random identifier is stored in your browser so the app can work properly on your device.

3. How we use information

We use your information to create and secure your account, show you your saved items, bookings and orders, provide weather, navigation and AI Guide features, and keep the app working and safe. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run Incaba. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: authentication and database.
  • Google: optional sign-in.
  • Groq: generates AI Guide replies.
  • Vercel: hosting.
  • A weather data provider: receives the place or coordinates needed to return a forecast.

When you make a booking or order with a business listed on Incaba, the details needed to fulfil it (for example your name and contact details) are shared with that business. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

5. Google user data

Incaba's use of information received from Google is limited to your basic profile (name, email address and profile picture) for signing you in and showing your name in the app. We do not share it with others except the providers listed above, and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account is active. To have your account and associated data deleted, email dlamininontokozo744@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will process the request.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with reputable providers. No online service can be guaranteed completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Children

Incaba is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

10. Contact
